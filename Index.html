❓ How to use CAPTION

1. Starting a Call: Share your ID with a friend. They can enter it in the "Join" box to connect.

2. Sign Language: Toggle "Sign: ON" and ensure your hands are visible in the camera for real-time translation.

3. Voice-to-Text: Enable "Voice: ON" to see live captions of what is being spoken.

4. Installation: Use the "Download App" button on the splash screen to add CAPTION to your home screen for offline use.

ℹ️ About CAPTION

CAPTION is a real-time communication tool designed to bridge the gap between ASL users and non-signers.

Using advanced AI (TensorFlow.js) and Peer-to-Peer technology, we provide instant sign-to-text and speech-to-text translation directly in your browser.

Key Features:

  • ASL Gesture Recognition
  • Bi-directional Speech Captioning
  • Encrypted Video Calling
  • Downloadable Mobile Experience
